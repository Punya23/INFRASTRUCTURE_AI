What we measure

Access

Distance to national highways, metro and railway stations, and how strong the main-road network is.

We measure road network capacity, not pavement condition.

Momentum

How much of the land became built-up between 2000 and 2020.

What this is not

It is not a forecast, a price prediction or financial advice. Scores describe infrastructure that exists today and growth that has already happened.
