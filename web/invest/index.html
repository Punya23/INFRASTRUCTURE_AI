What we measure

Five factors from open data that covers every city in India, in two groups.

Access

How easily people and goods get in and out.

Highways
Distance to the nearest national highway or expressway.
Metro and rail
Distance to the nearest metro station and the nearest railway station.
Road network
Kilometres of main road per square kilometre. We measure road network capacity, not pavement condition.

Momentum

How much the place has already grown.

Built-up growth
Change in the share of built-up land from 2000 to 2020, measured from satellite data.

Each area is a hexagon of about 5 km². A factor we could not measure is left out and shown as a dash, never counted as zero.

What this is not

  • Not a forecast

    We describe what exists today and what has already changed. We do not predict what happens next.

  • Not a price guide

    There are no prices, rents or valuations here, because no open source covers them for every city.

  • Not financial advice

    Use it to shortlist places to visit and check for yourself. Speak to a registered adviser before you commit money.

Sources and licenses

Open data only. Every score keeps the source, date and license of the facts behind it.
