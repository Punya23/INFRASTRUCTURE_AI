Methodology & Data Sources

Every score is a weighted sum of things that can be observed on the ground, and every number on this page is read live from the same API that feeds the maps. Scores describe existing infrastructure and past growth. They are not forecasts, price predictions or financial advice.

1. How an area score is computed

ADR-0003

The map is cut into hexagonal cells (H3 resolution 7, about 5 km² each). For each cell we measure five facts. Each fact is turned into a 0–100 sub-score along fixed, published break points, with straight-line interpolation between them. The area score is the weighted sum of its sub-scores. A city score is the population-weighted average of its areas. No model is trained and nothing is predicted (ADR-0003).

Specification
Score = Σ weightf × subscoref (weights sum to 1; unobserved facts are dropped and the rest re-scaled)

Also used outside the scores

  • National highways (NH Map): OpenStreetMap segments, with MoRTH Annual Report 2024-25 for official length, NHAI data for construction status and RAI 2024 for deaths. Each figure on that page names its source.
  • Upcoming projects (maps and city pages): news headlines found for each city. We store the headline, a short quote and the link, never the article, and the copyright stays with the publisher (ADR-0012, ADR-0016). A project is placed at the city centre because the article names no site.

3. What these numbers do not tell you

ADR-0003
Past and present, not the future

Growth is the change in built-up land between 2000 and 2020. A high score means infrastructure already exists and land has already grown. It does not predict prices or returns.

Modelled and community data

Population is a modelled 1 km grid for 2020, built-up land is satellite-derived, and roads and stations come from OpenStreetMap, which is community-maintained and uneven. Confidence starts at 80% and is lowered when a fact is missing for an area.

Uneven coverage

Bus and metro routes are drawn only where a public GTFS feed exists (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune so far). Elsewhere the score uses highways, rail, metro stations and roads only. Project news covers the cities where headlines were found, so an empty list does not mean nothing is planned.

No resident data yet

Citizen requests, complaints and ward budgets are planned but not collected, so they play no part in any score today. When they arrive they will be aggregates only, with counts below 5 suppressed, and never phone numbers, names or Aadhaar (ADR-0011).

Where the weights come from

Break points and preset weights are team judgment dated 2026-09-29, written next to each number in config/scoring.yaml. They are choices, not laws of nature. The four presets exist so you can see how the ranking moves when priorities change.
