1. Prioritization Scoring Algorithm (ADR-0003)

[Ref: ADR-0003] [Ref: Govt-Project-Registry]

Capital allocation across urban local bodies, state highway authorities, and railway boards in India has historically suffered from competing anecdotal priorities and lack of transparent criteria. In compliance with ADR-0003 (Explainable Analytics Only), INFRA-AI uses a deterministic, configuration-weighted mathematical formula evaluated directly from observable ground facts.

Mathematical Specification
Priority Score = (Complaint Volume [C] × Population Exposed [P] × Infrastructure Deficit Gap [G]) ÷ (Existing Sanctioned Budget [B] + 1)
C Complaint Volume
[Ref: PCMC-Grievance-2025]

What it measures: The volume of geotagged citizen grievances, safety flags, and ward petitions lodged across municipal portals and national redressal pipelines (CPGRAMS).

Why it matters: Captures ground reality and human friction (severe bottlenecks, lack of safe pedestrian crossings, delayed construction) that macro master plans overlook.

P Population Exposed
[Ref: Census-2011-PCA]

What it measures: Resident and daily transit commuter catchment population mapped to the official Local Government Directory (LGD) ward polygon.

Why it matters: Weights civic interventions by democratic scale, ensuring that critical arterial corridors serving tens of thousands of citizens receive proportionate priority.

G Infrastructure Deficit Gap
[Ref: Mission-Antyodaya-2024]

What it measures: A normalized decimal index from 0.00 (fully served) to 1.00 (complete absence of facility) based on MoRD and NITI Aayog norms.

Why it matters: Distinguishes between routine surface maintenance requests (G = 0.50) versus acute life-safety hazards where zero facilities exist (G = 0.92).

B Sanctioned Budget (+1 Scale)
[Ref: Govt-Project-Registry]

What it measures: Publicly sanctioned outlay in ₹ Crore from state works programmes, municipal budget books, and gazette notifications.

The (+1) Denominator Principle: Adding 1 prevents division-by-zero errors when an asset has zero budget sanctioned (B = ₹0.00 Cr). Crucially, this ensures neglected, unfunded ground needs receive the maximum mathematical priority boost.

Worked Benchmark Examples from Platform Registry:

Baptist Hospital Pedestrian Crossing (Bengaluru, KA): [Ref: PCMC-Grievance-2025]
(56 complaints × 48,000 pop × 0.92 gap) ÷ (₹0.00 Cr + 1) = 2,472,960.00 Priority Score
Hadapsar Transit Corridor Feeder Network (Pune, MH): [Ref: PMC-Sanction-2024]
(83 complaints × 125,000 pop × 0.78 gap) ÷ (₹4.50 Cr + 1) = 1,471,363.64 Priority Score
Hebbal Flyover Capacity Augmentation (Bengaluru, KA): [Ref: BDA-Gazette-2026]
(142 complaints × 185,000 pop × 0.85 gap) ÷ (₹380.00 Cr + 1) = 58,607.61 Priority Score

3. Cross-Lingual Semantic Clustering

[Ref: ADR-0008]

India is multilingual by definition. When citizens report ground issues, they do so in diverse languages including English, Hindi, and Kannada. Traditional civic portals fragment these reports into siloed queues, obscuring the true magnitude of an issue.

How Clustering Works:

  1. Acoustic & Text Ingest: Citizen voice inputs and text reports are captured in their native tongue via the browser's built-in Web Speech API.
  2. Dense Semantic Embedding: Cross-lingual sentence embeddings map complaints across languages into a unified semantic vector space, identifying shared intent regardless of syntax.
  3. Hierarchical Density Clustering: Geotagged reports within an LGD ward buffer are clustered into canonical issue groups (e.g., merging English reports of "45-min bottleneck queues", Hindi reports of "एक घंटे का जाम", and Kannada reports of "ತೀವ್ರ ಸಂಚಾರ ದಟ್ಟಣೆ").
  4. Verbatim Provenance Retention: In compliance with ADR-0008, the system never summarizes away the original claim. Every canonical cluster retains the verbatim original snippets for full auditability.

4. Methodological Limitations & Transparency Disclosures

[Ref: ADR-0003]
Showcase Demonstration Scope:

Current demonstration data spans three showcase metropolitan corridors: Bengaluru, Karnataka (Hebbal & North Corridor), Pune, Maharashtra (Hadapsar & Mula-Mutha Basin), and Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh (Kisan Path & Outer Ring). Field expansion across all 812 urban local bodies follows the field-first roadmap outlined in ADR-0013.

Census Demographic Baseline:

Population exposure figures [Ref: Census-2011-PCA] reflect the latest official decennial Census Primary Census Abstract, supplemented by ward-level Mission Antyodaya projections. Once Census 2026 figures are officially notified, the denominator joins will be updated automatically via LGD codes.

Zero Black-Box Predictive Modeling:

INFRA-AI deliberately rejects predictive deep learning or opaque regression scoring for public works ranking. All priority scores are deterministic arithmetic sums computed exclusively from verifiable public records.

Privacy by Default (ADR-0011):

No personally identifiable information (Aadhaar, phone numbers, or residential addresses) is stored. All citizen inputs are aggregated anonymously, and reporting bins with fewer than 5 submissions are suppressed to prevent re-identification.
